# Legal & Regulatory Compliance Guide: Truncate IDE

Navigating the global legal landscape is critical for the release of Truncate IDE, especially as an AI-powered tool handling sensitive enterprise data. This document outlines the mandatory requirements for a global rollout.

---

## 1. Global Data Privacy Foundations

### 🇪🇺 European Union (GDPR)
*   **Privacy by Design**: Mandatory Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for high-risk AI processing.
*   **Data Sovereignty**: Ensure local AI execution adheres to "Local Storage" mandates if applicable to specific member states.
*   **User Rights**: Support for "Right to be Forgotten" (deletion of local logs) and "Right to Portability."

### 🇺🇸 United States (CCPA/CPRA)
*   **Opt-Out Mechanisms**: While Truncate is local-first, any telemetry or crash reporting must have clear opt-outs.
*   **Transparency**: Explicitly disclose what metadata (if any) is collected and how it is used.

### 🇧🇷 Brazil (LGPD) & 🇨🇳 China (PIPL)
*   Mandatory appointment of a local Data Protection Officer (DPO) or representative for high-volume deployments in these regions.

---

## 2. The EU AI Act (Categorization)

Truncate IDE's AI features must be classified under the EU's risk-based framework:

*   **High-Risk Category**: If used in "Critical Infrastructure" or "Healthcare" decision pathways, Truncate must implement:
    *   Robust risk management systems.
    *   Strict data governance for any fine-tuning data.
    *   Detailed technical documentation and human oversight mechanisms.
*   **Limited-Risk (Transparency)**: AI features must clearly state they are AI-generated (e.g., "SQL generated by AI").

---

## 3. Sector-Specific Compliance

### 🏥 Healthcare (HIPAA)
*   **Business Associate Agreements (BAAs)**: Required if Truncate services handle Protected Health Information (PHI) for US entities.
*   **Technical Safeguards**: Enforce encryption at rest for local audit logs and strictly local model execution for PHI.

### 🏦 Banking & Finance
*   **Auditability (Basel III / SOX)**: Implement immutable, timestamped logs of all data cleaning and transformation steps to satisfy regulatory audits.
*   **Non-Repudiation**: Ensure query logs cannot be modified once written.

### 🪖 Military & Defense (Export Controls)
*   **ITAR/EAR Compliance**: Advanced AI model weights and specific "Dual-Use" technology must comply with US Department of Commerce export licenses before shipping to "Tier 3" countries (e.g., China, Russia).
*   **Air-Gapped Validation**: Must undergo security certification for air-gapped environment usage.

---

## 4. Software Licensing & Intellectual Property

*   **EULA (End User License Agreement)**:
    *   **Liability Waiver**: Explicitly disclaim liability for AI-hallucinated SQL that leads to data loss.
    *   **Data Ownership**: Guarantee that the user owns all generated SQL and processed datasets.
*   **Open Source Attribution**: Maintain a `credits.md` or `NOTICE` file listing all bundled open-source licenses (e.g., MIT/Apache licenses for AI libraries).

---

| :--- | :--- |

| **Australia** | Adherence to the **Voluntary AI Ethics Framework** and Privacy Act 1988. |

---

## 6. Pre-Release Compliance Strategy

1.  **Conduct a DPIA**: Perform a formal Data Protection Impact Assessment on local AI processing.
2.  **Draft a BAA Template**: Have a standard Business Associate Agreement ready for healthcare clients.
3.  **Implement Logging Toggles**: Allow users to explicitly turn on/off all local logging for "Stealth Mode" operations.
4.  **License Disclosure**: Finalize a full audit of all third-party dependencies.
